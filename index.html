Lab 1: Volleyball Basics

In this lab, we will learn the basics of volleyball, including the basic skills necessary for a rally.

In order to play volleyball, you need to know the basic moves.

These moves include:.

  • Serving
  • Passing
  • Setting
  • Hitting
  • Defending
  • Blocking

As you can see, there are many skills that are necessary to play volleyball. The attached video will show a rally with each of these skills. Watch this rally!

Volleyball is also a fast-paced sport that depends on teamwork, communication, and strong fundamentals.

Thank you for reading this lab. I hope you learned something new about volleyball!
