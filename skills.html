Essential Volleyball Skills

Volleyball requires serving, passing, setting, and hitting for a play.

Skill Purpose Difficulty
Serving Starts the rally Medium
Passing Controls the ball Medium
Setting Sets up the hitter Hard
Hitting Produces the offense Hard

The first skill we will cover is serving.

Serving is the only skill in volleyball that a player has full control of, and takes a lot of mental strength.

The second skill is passing.

Passing is very technical, and works with angles, ball velocity, and footwork.

The third is setting.

Setting takes a lot of skill and time to master. It is also the position that runs the offense.

And finally, hitting.

Hitting is one of the most fun skills in the game, there is plenty of technique involved, but it is the skill that has the most variety.
